# Creative style library

These 12 names and descriptions match the current workspace style catalog. Styles guide composition and prompt preparation. They are not model releases, rendered previews or proof that an image or video has been generated.

| Style | Direction | Example use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clean product studio** | Soft studio light, uncluttered background and accurate product materials. | A product launch hero with generous space for a headline |
| **Cyberpunk** | Neon lighting, futuristic atmosphere and bold contrast. | A futuristic technology campaign with dramatic neon accents |
| **Editorial** | Magazine-like composition with a strong visual story. | A report cover or thought-leadership campaign |
| **Brand minimal** | Simple shapes, calm colors and a clear message. | A professional social announcement or corporate cover |
| **3D concept** | Sculptural forms, depth and polished materials. | A conceptual product or service illustration |
| **Cinematic** | Film lighting, deliberate framing and atmospheric depth. | A dramatic campaign key visual or video opening |
| **Flat illustration** | Friendly shapes and clear, readable storytelling. | An onboarding guide or explainer graphic |
| **Watercolor** | Hand-painted textures and a softer emotional tone. | A lifestyle campaign or warm editorial illustration |
| **Technical diagram** | Precise construction and clearly separated components. | A product explainer or engineering overview |
| **Luxury** | Refined contrast, rich materials and restrained highlights. | A high-end launch announcement |
| **Playful** | Expressive color, lively shapes and a welcoming tone. | A consumer promotion or event announcement |
| **Documentary** | Natural light, believable settings and human context. | A team story or customer case study |

## From an idea to a brief

Clarify the subject, product facts, intended audience, brand constraints, aspect ratio, headline and desired style. Offer understandable alternatives when the request is ambiguous. Actual preview/generation needs an available generator and suitable assets; text preparation alone is not an image or video.
